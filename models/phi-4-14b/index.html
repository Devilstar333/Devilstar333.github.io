The short answer

At Q4_K_M (the quantization most people run) Phi-4 14B needs about 11.6 GB of memory at a 16384-token context, so you want a 16 GB GPU to keep it entirely on the GPU.

On a 12 GB card it does not fit: roughly 11.2 GB stay in VRAM and 0.4 GB spill to system RAM. Offload works, but generation slows down several times, because system RAM bandwidth is far below GPU bandwidth. Dropping the context to 4,096 tokens frees about 1.2 GB.
